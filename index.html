Our mission

Three things we set out to do

  1. Tear down walls

    that cancer builds emotionally and socially for teens.

  2. Provide a spark

    of VIP treatment during key life phases.

  3. Pave the way

    for a future of socially minded, personally responsible community and business leaders.

Our story

It started with one teen and a weekend

Our “for teens, from teens” programming model has allowed Heart of Passion to connect teens living with cancer to each other to form an emotional support structure in an environment that only teens themselves could create. Simultaneously, our teen leaders personally experience the value and purpose of their work and compassion.

Our programs

The red carpet, rolled out

Red Carpet Day

Through our Red Carpet Day (RCD) retreats, we provide positive unique experiences for teens with cancer to anticipate, enjoy, and make memories to share: positive memories to help balance out the negative uniqueness that cancer brings to their everyday lives.

Donate

From the minute a guest starts the trip until the minute their parents pick them up, HoP covers every meal, activity, hotel room, and surprise. Donations are tax deductible.

Ways to give

Mentor

Mentor teams of 3 to 5 young professionals challenge and support a cohort of 15 teen leaders while the leaders plan their summer Red Carpet Day retreat completely on their own.

Be an adult mentor

Lead

The Leadership Development Program is an intense year-long program for exceptionally motivated, passionate high school students. The leader cohort plans and fundraises for a summer Red Carpet Retreat for teens with cancer.
