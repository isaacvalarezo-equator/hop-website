    How the Heart Meter works

    Ways money comes in

    • Your Spotfund page. Your own fundraiser under Heart Of Passion Inc. Donors give there and the money goes to HoP. Once your mentor links your page, the Heart Meter reads it every hour and your heart shows "on Spotfund". Until then, log each Spotfund gift here and pick "Spotfund" under "How it came in". After the link, those gifts stay on the list but are not counted twice.
    • A company sponsorship. Log the business in Outreach first. When they give, log the donation and pick "Company sponsorship".
    • An in-kind donation. Goods or services instead of money. Log it with "In-kind donation" and ask your mentor what value to use.
    • A fundraiser or event. Giving Tuesday, Instagram Bingo, Speed Networking, a giveback night. Pick the event under "How it came in" so it shows up in By event.
    • Anything else. A grant, a leader's own gift, or another way. Pick the closest option and explain in the note.

    What you can do here

    • Log a donation. Your heart and the team total fill up right away.
    • Tick "HoP already received it" once the money is with HoP, and "Thank-you sent" once you thank the donor.
    • Log outreach. Every business or sponsor you contact, with your next step. Move the card as they answer, and update the next step when it changes.
    • Tap a heart to see only that leader's gifts. By event shows how much each fundraiser brought in.
    • Fix a mistake with the delete button. Every change is saved in a log, so nothing is lost.

    Good to know

    • The team code comes from your mentor. Do not share it outside the team.
    • Do not write phone numbers or emails in notes. Anyone with this link can read them.
    • Participation points. In the Points tab, log each task you complete: who you worked with, how long it took, what you learned, what you would do differently, and a photo, video or link. A mentor approves it and the points count. Each contact you log in Outreach also shows up there for a mentor to approve.
